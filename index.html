04 / INVARIANTS Non-negotiable engineering axioms

Rules we do not bend.

AXIOM 01

Native over simulated

Never disguise a flattened approximation as working structure. Construct real, native objects — and surface honest boundaries where unsolved residuals remain.

AXIOM 02

First principles over patches

Every rule must generalize from optical physics, pixel geometry, and domain-agnostic semantics — never hardcoded special cases that overfit a benchmark.

AXIOM 03

Preserve unselected human work

Scoped edits must respect locked and unselected elements without collateral mutation. Intelligence assists the author; it never overwrites their craft.

AXIOM 04

Verify the whole artifact

Aggregate similarity scores can hide broken details. Acceptance requires independent, end-to-end validation of the actual delivered output in its native environment.
